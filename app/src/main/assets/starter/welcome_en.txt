Welcome to BookQuest

This is a short book that explains how your library works.
Read it now, or come back to it whenever you like.


Chapter one: what this quest is

BookQuest is a library that you build yourself.
Every book you add gets its own colourful cover, a school subject and a progress bar.
The more you read, the more points you earn and the higher your level climbs.


Chapter two: adding books

1. Tap the big plus button on the home or library screen.
2. Pick one file, or several at once, from your device.
3. The book is copied into the app, so you can read it with no internet at all.

Supported formats are TXT, PDF, EPUB, HTML and MD.
Your teacher can send you a file by email or chat, and you simply add it here.


Chapter three: points, streaks and badges

Every minute of reading is worth three points.
Finishing a book is worth fifty points.
Every two hundred points moves you up a level.

Read on two days in a row and a streak begins.
Three days unlocks a badge, seven days unlocks a rarer one.


Chapter four: flashcards and quizzes

Every book has room for flashcards.
Write a question on one side and the answer on the other,
then play the quiz and find out what really stuck.

It is the fastest way to study for a test:
read, write three questions, play.


Chapter five: three languages

This app speaks Hebrew, English and Arabic.
Change the language any time from the settings screen,
and the whole layout flips between right to left and left to right for you.

Try all three languages and a special badge is yours.


The end

Your turn now. Add your first book and start reading.
Enjoy!
